# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

KernelScope is a defensive security and systems-engineering project. We are
committed to a professional, inclusive, and technically rigorous community in
which participants can contribute without harassment, intimidation, or abuse.

We expect every participant to act with respect, assume good faith, separate
technical criticism from personal criticism, and prioritize the safety of
people who may test kernel code.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Explain security and engineering claims with reproducible evidence.
- Welcome corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect private information, credentials, signing material, and sensitive dumps.
- Follow the project's defensive scope and documented-API policy.
- Warn clearly before suggesting actions that can crash, boot-loop, or weaken a system.
- Keep vulnerability reports private until coordinated disclosure is complete.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately unsafe instructions presented without appropriate warnings.
- Requests or contributions for stealth, persistence, credential collection,
  security-feature bypasses, arbitrary memory access, hiding, or offensive evasion.
- Uploading malware, private keys, credentials, proprietary drivers, or sensitive dumps.
- Knowingly misrepresenting test results, security properties, or release readiness.
- Disrupting discussions, reviews, issue triage, or coordinated disclosure.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release
coordination, private vulnerability reports, and public spaces where someone is
representing the KernelScope project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, lock
discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct
violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented,
and focused on protecting the community and project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If a concern
also involves a security vulnerability, use the private process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not publish personal or sensitive information
in a public issue.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by widely used open-source community standards,
including the Contributor Covenant, while being tailored to the safety and
defensive-scope requirements of kernel security research.
